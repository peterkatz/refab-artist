# Refab Artist 0.1.0

The first release of Refab Artist: a native macOS app for Apple silicon that
turns a 3D model into flat parts for laser, CNC or hand cutting, with assembly
steps, plus tools for 3D printing and casting.

Refab Artist is released under the GNU General Public License, version 3. See
the [README](../../README.md) for what the app does and how to build it.

## What is in 0.1.0

- Ten construction modes, one of them experimental (the table below).
- Make Watertight, Hollow and Thicken for repairing and reshaping a model
  before you slice it, without changing the file you imported.
- Material and sheet presets, kerf and slot offsets, a tool diameter for
  router relief, and a Slice Direction tilt.
- Cut-sheet export as DXF, SVG, PDF or EPS; part export as STL or OBJ; a
  printable assembly guide; project files (`.refabartist`).
- An optional 3Dconnexion SpaceMouse for moving around the 3D view.

## Modes and their status

Each mode is listed as one of: **supported** (passes the checks named in the
last column), **supported with known limits** (the limits are under Known
issues below), or **experimental**. No mode is intentionally unavailable.

| Mode | Output | Status | Checked by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stacked | Cut sheets; STL, OBJ | Supported | Functional gate (10 cases), assembly gate |
| Interlocked | Cut sheets; STL, OBJ | Supported | Functional gate (5 cases; slot fit, not joint mating), assembly gate, organic-model gate |
| Radial | Cut sheets; STL, OBJ | Supported | Functional gate (17 cases), assembly gate, organic-model gate |
| Curve | Cut sheets; STL, OBJ | Supported with known limits | Functional gate (38 cases), assembly gate, organic-model gate |
| Folded | Cut sheets (flat nets); STL, OBJ | Supported with known limits | Functional gate (58 cases), assembly gate |
| 3D Slices | STL, OBJ (layered mesh, no cut sheet) | Supported | Swift tests |
| Structures | STL, OBJ | Supported | Swift tests |
| Partition | STL, OBJ, named OBJ, plate; assembly map | Supported with known limits | Swift tests (Split by Undercuts on simple shapes only) |
| MotherMold | STL, ZIP, plate; assembly map | Supported with known limits | Swift tests |
| Make Watertight, Hollow, Thicken | Applied to the model before any mode | Supported | Swift tests |
| Inflatables | Cut sheets (flat panels); STL, OBJ (the inflated shape) | Experimental | Swift tests |

The gates are described in [docs/functional-gate.md](../functional-gate.md).
They check that exported parts can be cut and assembled, not that they fit your
material.

**Inflatables is experimental.** It is hidden until you turn on **Show
experimental modes** in Settings. It is still in development; do not rely on it.

## Requirements and installing

- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). Intel Macs are not supported.
- macOS 15 or newer. 0.1.0 was built and checked on macOS 26.4 only; it has not
  been run on macOS 15 or 14.
- Download from the [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/peterkatz/refab-artist/releases),
  or build it from source (see the README).
- The app does not update itself. Download new versions from the Releases page.

**Signing.** The download is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple,
with the ticket stapled, so it opens normally. The file is
`Refab-Artist-0.1.0.zip` (SHA-256 `ffb305550626f103f0cce18a4853be42a5e3aa46db2292d26013fd5edf214e59`).

**SpaceMouse.** The app loads 3Dconnexion's driver library at run time, so it
is signed with the entitlement that allows a third-party library to load (see
[docs/signed-notarized-build.md](../signed-notarized-build.md)). A probe signed
the same way loads the installed driver; without the entitlement macOS refuses
it.

## How 0.1.0 was checked

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Swift tests (XCTest) | 361 run: 348 passed (one of them an expected failure for a known issue below), 13 skipped, 0 failed |
| Swift tests (swift-testing) | 805 tests in 111 suites passed, with 5 known issues in 3 tests (below) |
| Python tests | 497 passed |
| Functional gate | 128 of 128 cases pass (Stacked 10, Interlocked 5, Radial 17, Curve 38, Folded 58); regenerating the exports reproduces the committed files byte for byte |
| Assembly gate | 22 of 22 cases pass |
| Organic-model gate | 3 of 3 pass: a scanned hand through Radial, Interlocked and Curve. The optional slow case needs a large scan that is not in the repository and was not run |
| Physical cut test | **Not done.** No parts have been cut and assembled in real material for 0.1.0 |

Of the 13 skipped tests, 11 are opt-in checks that an environment variable
switches on. The other 2 are the Split by Undercuts checks on a scanned hand and
an open mask; they skip because their expected results have not been recorded
yet (Known issues, Partition).

**What this does not show.** The gates show that exported files are consistent
and cuttable. Nothing here shows that parts fit your material: kerf, stock
thickness and machine all matter. Cut a small test piece first and adjust **Slot
Offset** until the joints fit; [docs/physical-calibration.md](../physical-calibration.md)
describes a test piece.

## Known issues

### Cut-sheet modes

- **Curve, first axis Y, 5 mm stock.** On some organic models a few parts are
  marked (blue on the cut sheet) to be glued in place, instead of being held by
  joints. Another first axis, fewer curve slices or thinner stock join them.
- **Curve, ribs moved to join parts.** A first-axis rib that the planner moves
  off its even spacing to join parts can leave a small lip near flat faces.
- **Slice Direction and STL / OBJ.** With a tilted Slice Direction, the STL and
  OBJ files are written in the tilted frame: the whole assembly comes out turned
  by the tilt. The viewport and the cut sheets are not affected.
- **Radial fin count.** Asking for 8 fins gives 10.
- **Interlocked and the joint check.** Interlocked exports carry no part frames
  (where each part sits in the model), so the functional gate checks slot widths
  and cuttability on Interlocked but cannot check that every pair of slots mates.
- **Interlocked, thick stock on a small model.** If the stock is as thick as the
  slice spacing, the slots merge and each part loses its slotted half. Nothing
  warns about it; set **Distribute count** to fix it.
- **Radial.** Ring pieces that no fin reaches are left out, and the notes name
  them. Pieces that meet the other family only where a line crosses the model
  more than once are marked to be glued, not locked.
- **Curve.** Where the curve bends sharply, the curve ribs stop short of the
  concave side, so that side is held by the first-axis ribs alone. A few ribs
  have thin webs, and notes name them.
- **Folded.** It assumes the stock bends at the fold lines (card, thin sheet, or
  a perforated or kerf-cut crease); in stiff stock the fold lines must be cut
  through and glued. On curved models most edges are left plain to glue or tape.
  Assembly step numbers count panels while the cut sheet has one part per net,
  so they differ from the sheet labels.
- **Assembly order** is not checked for Curve and Folded: a joint is checked
  where the parts end up, not that every part can be slid into place in turn.

[docs/specs/](../specs/) lists these in more detail under each mode.

### Partition

- **Split by Undercuts on scanned models is not checked yet.** The tests check
  the undercut measurement on simple shapes and on the scanned hand and mask,
  and the split itself on simple shapes. The two tests that split the scanned
  hand and the open mask and check the pieces do not run: their expected
  results have not been recorded, so they are skipped. On an organic scan,
  check each piece in the 3D view before you mould it. **Fit Printer** is not
  affected.

### MotherMold

These are the failures that the tests record as known issues. Each test still
runs its real checks and will say so if it starts to pass.

- **Four-part reusable silicone mold on some models.** On the low-poly head test
  model the build stops while placing the liner registration buttons, because a
  quarter shell cannot spread them over two directions. No mold package is
  written.
- **Four-part mold of a hand scan with X and Z planes through its centre.** Two
  of the quarter shells come out in two pieces each, and the planner refuses a
  mold part in two pieces. The X/Y and Y/Z plane pairs on the same scan work.
- **Prepared master of an already healed hand scan.** The automatic post patch
  keeps only about 97 percent of the round root footprint, so the prepared
  master is refused.
- **Pour funnel on a small mold.** The default 12 mm sealed pour funnel does not
  fit on one part of a 50 mm cube parted through its centre; a 10 mm one does.
  Choose a smaller spout on small molds.

### Other

- Physical cut tests are not done (above).
- Inflatables is experimental (above).
- The app has been run on macOS 26 only.
- At small sizes, such as Finder's list view, macOS 26 shows the app icon on a
  light-grey backdrop, as it does for other apps with an older-style icon.

## Not in 0.1.0

- No Intel, Windows or Linux build.
- No automatic update, no Homebrew cask and no Mac App Store release.
