# Glossary

**Assembly guide.** A printable list of every assembly step, with the model, mode and material settings. See [Exporting](exporting.md).

**Cut sheet.** The layout of all the flat parts on sheets of your stock, ready to export.

**Dog-bone relief.** A round cut-out at the inside corner of a slot so a round router bit can reach the corner and a square part can seat into it. Set with **Tool Dia.** and a relief type.

**Dowel.** A rod threaded through holes in every layer of a Stacked model to keep the layers lined up.

**Fin.** One of the vertical parts fanned around the centre in [Radial](radial.md).

**First-axis rib.** One of the parallel slices in [Curve](curve.md).

**Fold line.** A line on a Folded net where the stock bends. Drawn as a score or as perforation dashes.

**Half-lap joint.** A joint where each of two parts has a slot cut halfway through, so they cross flush. Used in Interlocked, Radial and Curve.

**Kerf.** The width of material a cutter removes. See [Cutting and fit](cutting-and-fit.md).

**Mode.** A construction technique: Stacked, Interlocked, Radial, Curve, Folded, 3D Slices, Structures, Partition, MotherMold or Inflatables.

**Net.** A group of panels laid flat in one piece, joined along fold lines, in Folded.

**Notch.** A cut into the edge of a part where another part slots in.

**Object Size.** The panel in the Object tab for checking and changing the units and size of the model.

**Panel.** A flat face of the model, made of triangles in one plane, in Folded.

**Perforate.** Draws Folded fold lines as cut dashes so thick stock folds easily.

**Relief.** See *dog-bone relief*.

**Ring.** One of the stacked cross-sections in [Radial](radial.md).

**Seam.** A place where two cut edges meet and are joined. In Partition, a place where two pieces meet and carry keys.

**Slice.** To compute the parts for the chosen mode. Also one layer of the model.

**Slice Direction.** The tilt of the cut away from the model's axes. It aims the cut; it does not turn the model.

**Slot Offset.** Extra width added to every slot so parts fit after the kerf. See [Cutting and fit](cutting-and-fit.md).

**Stock.** The sheet material you are cutting from.

**Tool Dia.** The diameter of the router bit used for relief cuts.

**Watertight.** A mesh with no holes: a closed surface. See [Model repair](model-repair.md).
